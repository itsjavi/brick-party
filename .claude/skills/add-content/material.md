# Topic material

What to write about, one section per topic in `packages/content/src/categories.ts`. The game is set in 2026 and
beyond, so write about work and technology as they look now and will look next. Office, startup and survey life are
the base, not the whole pack.

## formbricks

Formbricks is an open-source survey and experience-management product, and the people playing build it, so roast it
affectionately and accurately. Formbricks ships fast: before a batch of these, skim the current docs (the brief says
where they are) for new features and changed names, and check each joke against the real feature.

- **Surveys:** link surveys; website and in-app pop-ups with show conditions, actions and triggers (page views, clicks,
  exit intent); attribute-based targeting; showing a survey to a percentage of users; the recontact cooldown;
  scheduling; partial submissions; hidden fields; variables; recall (piping an earlier answer into a later question);
  conditional logic and branching; validation rules; multi-language surveys with AI translation; quotas; spam
  protection; custom CSS and themes; email follow-ups.
- **Question types:** NPS, CSAT, CES (customer effort score), matrix, ranking, picture select, rating, free text, file
  upload, schedule a meeting, address, contact info, consent, date, statement or call to action, and the thank-you
  card.
- **Links:** single-use links, personal links, PIN-protected surveys, verifying an email before a survey, prefilled
  data, offline support, embedded surveys.
- **Methods:** the product-market-fit survey ("How would you feel if you could no longer use...?", where "very
  disappointed" is the answer you want), feature chasers, headless surveys.
- **Platform:** contacts, segments and attributes; teams and roles; 2FA and SSO; integrations (Slack, Notion, HubSpot,
  Salesforce, Airtable, Google Sheets, Zapier, Make, n8n, webhooks and more); the "Powered by Formbricks" badge,
  which only the Enterprise plan can hide; an MCP server so AI agents can use Formbricks; importing surveys from a
  big legacy survey tool.
- **Open source and self-hosting:** the GitHub repository and its stars, issues and contributors; self-hosting with
  Docker or Kubernetes, a long list of environment variables, license keys, upgrades.
- **Unify Feedback:** feedback records gathered from many sources, topics and subtopics, enrichment, semantic search,
  dashboards and charts that AI builds from one sentence, journey scorecards, detection signals, "closing the loop".
- **Workflows** that act on feedback automatically.

## ai

From brilliant to catastrophic: AI agents acting on their own and booking meetings with other agents; AI coworkers,
managers and interviewers; jobs taken over; prompt engineering as a career; confident hallucinations; surveys written,
answered and analyzed by AI with no human involved; thanking chatbots as insurance for the uprising; humans kept as
batteries or living in a simulation; robot overlords with HR policies; a post-scarcity utopia that still has a
standup; GPU shortages and data centers drinking lakes; AI aligned to the quarterly targets.

## product

Roadmaps and their "Now / Next / Later" columns, MVPs, feature requests, feature flags, A/B tests, North Star metrics,
vanity metrics, launches nobody notices, sunsetting features, stakeholders, user interviews, the customer who asked for
a faster horse.

## ux

Dark patterns and confirm-shaming, cookie banners, onboarding tours, modals on modals, design systems with 40 shades of
grey, "make the logo bigger", infinite scroll, settings pages, error messages, progress bars, the push door you pull.

## accessibility

Roast inaccessible products, design decisions and the companies behind them, never people with disabilities: grey text
on grey, alt text that says "image", keyboard traps, focus outlines removed as "ugly", "click here" links, captchas
only robots pass, autoplay with no captions, accessibility overlays that make things worse, accessibility planned for
"phase two" since forever, a ramp that leads to stairs.

## engineering

Vibe coding; AI-written pull requests approved by another AI; legacy code from 2025 that nobody understands; flaky
tests; deploy freezes; Friday deploys; incident retros and blameless postmortems; dependencies maintained by one tired
stranger; YAML; the cloud; "works on my machine"; on-call.

## office, startup, surveys

Meetings and calls, chat etiquette, coworkers and managers, the shared kitchen, return-to-office, reorgs; pricing,
sales, investors, churn, pivots, growth hacks; surveys, ratings and feedback forms everywhere in life.

## life and surreal

Everyday life everyone shares (food, home, travel, family, hobbies, chores), and pure nonsense: creatures, objects and
cosmic things living absurd lives with no real-world target.
