## Context

The team already plays on a video call. Built-in voice was offered and the user chose the call.

## Decision

The game has text chat and reaction buttons, each with its own sound, but no voice.

## Consequences

- No WebRTC or voice service.
- Bot or Not's discussion happens on the call and in chat.
